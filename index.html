🎯 Matches / Differs Analyzer

Matches: the next digit equals the selected digit.
Differs: the next digit is anything except the selected digit.

A Match naturally occurs around 1 in 10 times for a balanced digit process, while Differ occurs around 9 in 10 times. The contract payout therefore matters.

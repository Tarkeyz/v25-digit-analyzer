Important

This tool tests a statistical hypothesis. A low frequency of digits 0–3 does NOT mathematically force the next digit to be 4–9, and a low frequency of 7–9 does NOT force the next digit to be 0–6. Synthetic Indices are generated using Deriv's random-number generation system. Historical frequency is therefore treated here as data to test, not as a guaranteed prediction.
